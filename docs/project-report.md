# Kasanga Market Dues Revenue Leakage Detector

## 1. Project Title
Kasanga Market Dues Revenue Leakage Detector

## 2. Introduction
Kasanga Market is a busy trading place where traders pay regular dues to the local authority. In many markets, revenue is lost because of poor tracking, incomplete payment records, duplicate payments, unpaid balances, unregistered traders, and weak monitoring processes. This results in financial leakage and weak public financial management.

The proposed project is a system that helps local government officers record trader details, track due payments, detect missing revenue, and identify sources of leakage. The system will improve accountability, increase collected revenue, and simplify monitoring for local officials.

## 3. Problem Statement
The local government and market administration face challenges in collecting and monitoring dues from traders. Some traders do not pay on time, some underpay, some payments are missing from the ledger, and some unregistered traders operate without paying any dues. These issues lead to revenue leakage, which reduces the government’s income and affects local service delivery.

The market dues leakage detector will address this problem by tracking trader registration, dues, payments, and outstanding amounts in one system. It will generate alerts when payments are missing, overdue, duplicated, or inconsistent with expected market revenue.

## 4. Objectives of the Project
- To register all traders in Kasanga Market
- To track due amounts and payment history
- To identify unpaid and overdue dues
- To detect underpayments and duplicate receipts
- To compare expected revenue with collected revenue
- To highlight revenue leakage by market section, trader, or month
- To provide reports for managerial decisions
- To support local government revenue recovery actions

## 5. Scope of the System
The system will cover the following:
- Trader registration
- Stall assignment and business location
- Dues calculation
- Payment and receipt records
- Overdue and missing payment alerts
- Revenue leakage analysis
- Government dashboard report generation

## 6. Key Features
- Trader profile creation
- Stall identification and market section
- Due tracking per month
- Payment recording and receipt number validation
- Balance calculation
- Leakage detection logic
- Search and filter tools
- Monthly revenue reporting
- Dashboard summary for officers

## 7. Functional Requirements
- The system should allow officers to register traders.
- The system should store trader names, stall numbers, market section, and contact details.
- The system should record monthly dues and due dates.
- The system should accept payment entries and update balances.
- The system should identify unpaid and overdue dues.
- The system should prevent duplicate receipts or repeated payment entry.
- The system should compare expected revenue with actual revenue.
- The system should generate reports on leakage and outstanding balances.

## 8. Non-Functional Requirements
- User-friendly interface
- Data security and role-based access
- Accurate financial records
- Fast processing
- Reliable backups
- Usability for local government staff

For traders, it provides fairness because dues are tracked more clearly. The project is also valuable as a commercial product because it can be adapted and sold to other local councils and market authorities.

## 11. Revenue Leakage Logic
Expected Revenue = Total active traders × monthly due amount
Collected Revenue = actual amount received
Leakage = Expected Revenue − Collected Revenue

Example:
- Active traders: 120
- Monthly due per trader: 30,000 UGX
- Expected revenue: 3,600,000 UGX
- Actual collected: 2,900,000 UGX
- Leakage: 700,000 UGX
